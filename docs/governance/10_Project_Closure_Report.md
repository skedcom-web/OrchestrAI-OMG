## 1. Executive Summary & Accomplishments
The **OrchestrAI Model Governance (OMG)** enterprise platform has successfully completed **Phases 1 through 6** under the **OrchestrAI Delivery Framework (ODF v1.0)** governance standard.

OMG is fully deployed live in production at 🔗 **[https://orchestrai-omg.web.app](https://orchestrai-omg.web.app)** and connects to a serverless **Neon PostgreSQL** database and a **NestJS REST API**.

---

## 2. Summary of Delivered Modules (Phases 1 - 6)

### Phase 1: AI Asset Registry & Inventory
- Centralized inventory supporting 9 AI Asset Types with full lifecycle status tracking.

### Phase 2 & 2.5: Risk Management, RACIS Ownership & 7-Role RBAC
- Risk Assessment Wizard, 5-role RACIS Ownership Matrix, 1-click persona switching across 7 roles, and navigation RBAC.

### Phase 3: Validation & Evidence Repository
- Multi-discipline validation across 6 categories, evidence repository mapped to 10 ODF deliverables, Review Workbench, and Findings tracker.

### Phase 4: Decision Intelligence Center
- 5-Pillar Governance Score Engine (20% x 5 = 100 points), Readiness Tiers (GO/CONDITIONAL/NO GO), Governance Blockers Evaluator, Decision Authority Workbench, and Executive Briefing Package Generator.

### Phase 5: Compliance & Regulatory Intelligence Center
- Seeded RBI AI Governance Control Library (`RBI-001` to `RBI-008`), Compliance Assessment Engine (0-100%), Regulatory Library, Compliance Findings, and Audit-Ready Compliance Package Generator.

### Phase 6: Operational Governance & Kill Switch Center
- Operations Command Center (`Active`, `Suspended`, `Under Review`, `Planned`, `Retired`), Emergency Kill Switch Console, Human Override Tracker, Incident Management, Asset Retirement Workflow, and Governance Event Lifecycle Timeline.
