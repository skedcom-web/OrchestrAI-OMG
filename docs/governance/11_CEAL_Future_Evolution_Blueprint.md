## Document Summary & Context
This document assesses the current **OMG Governance Operating System** baseline (Phases 1-7) and defines a future evolution path: the **Capability & Evidence Assurance Layer (CEAL)**. 

CEAL establishes an independent upstream reference layer evaluated against:
1. **Application Domain & Permitted Consequence**
2. **Required Evidence Class (E0 to E7)**
3. **Computational Capability Taxonomy**
4. **Failure Boundary Registry**
5. **Execution Authority Scoping**
6. **Evidence Sufficiency Assessment**

Positioned as **Phase 10 (Advanced Assurance Extension)** for high-assurance sectors (*Banking, Defense, Healthcare, Critical Infrastructure*).
