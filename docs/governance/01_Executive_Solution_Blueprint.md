# ODF Deliverable 01: Executive Solution Blueprint
> **OrchestrAI Model Governance (OMG) Enterprise Platform**  
> **Framework Standard**: ODF Universal Governance Standard v1.0  
> **Version**: 1.0 (Phases 1 - 6 Approved Baseline)  

---

## 1. Business Problem & Context
Enterprises deploying AI Applications, Autonomous Agents, LLMs, Copilots, and RAG Systems face unprecedented risks surrounding ungoverned AI execution, lack of accountable ownership, unverified model accuracy, regulatory non-compliance (specifically **RBI AI Governance Standards**), and absence of operational emergency suspension (Kill Switch) controls. 

Without a unified governance platform, enterprise leadership cannot answer:
- *"Who owns this AI system?"* (Ownership)
- *"Is the risk classified and mitigated?"* (Risk)
- *"Has it passed multi-disciplinary validation?"* (Validation)
- *"Is there audit-ready evidence for compliance?"* (Compliance & ODF)
- *"Can we safely suspend or override it in production?"* (Operations & Kill Switch)

---

## 2. Strategic Objectives
OrchestrAI Model Governance (OMG) delivers a 6-phase centralized governance command center that enforces:
1. **100% Centralized AI Inventory**: Full visibility over 9 distinct AI asset classes.
2. **RACIS Accountable Ownership Matrix**: 5 mandatory ownership roles per asset.
3. **Multi-Discipline Validation & Evidence Repository**: 10 ODF mandatory deliverables tracked.
4. **5-Pillar Decision Intelligence Engine**: Automated 0-100% readiness scoring.
5. **RBI AI Governance Alignment**: Automated compliance evaluation against Controls RBI-001 through RBI-008.
6. **Production Runtime Control**: Emergency Kill Switch, Human Override logging, and controlled Decommissioning.

---

## 3. Project Scope
The OMG platform scope encompasses 6 fully implemented functional pillars:
- **Phase 1**: AI Asset Registry & Inventory Management
- **Phase 2**: Risk Management & Classification Wizard
- **Phase 2.5**: Identity, Persona Switching & 7-Role RBAC
- **Phase 3**: Multi-Disciplinary Validation, Evidence Repository & Findings Tracker
- **Phase 4**: Decision Intelligence Center & 5-Pillar Readiness Scoring (GO/CONDITIONAL/NO GO)
- **Phase 5**: Compliance & Regulatory Intelligence Center (RBI Control Library & Audit Packages)
- **Phase 6**: Operational Governance, Emergency Kill Switch, Human Override & Asset Retirement
