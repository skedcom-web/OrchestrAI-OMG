# OMG Master Blueprint v2.0
## OrchestrAI AI Governance Command Center

### Consolidated Phase-1 + Phase-2 + Decision Governance Update

# Vision
OMG (OrchestrAI Model Governance) is an enterprise AI Governance Command Center.

Know Your AI.
Govern Your AI.
Control Your AI.

# Core Problem Statement
Organizations are rapidly adopting:
- AI Applications
- AI Agents
- LLMs
- AI Models
- Copilots
- RAG Systems
- AI Workflows
- Multi-Agent Systems
- Third-Party AI Services

Most organizations cannot answer:
- What AI assets exist?
- Who owns them?
- Who approved them?
- Which assets are high risk?
- Which assets require review?
- Can assets be controlled or stopped?
- Are assets audit ready?

# Strategic Principle v2
AI Governance should not become Governance Debt.

OMG must always answer:
'Can this AI asset move forward today?'
