# 1. Executive Summary

OrchestrAI Enterprise Suite consists of two complementary frameworks:

## ODF
OrchestrAI Delivery Framework

Purpose:
Build AI-powered solutions rapidly.

## OMG
OrchestrAI Model Governance

Purpose:
Govern AI assets across the enterprise.

Together:

OMG Governs
→ ODF Delivers
→ OMG Monitors
→ ODF Improves

This creates a complete enterprise AI lifecycle.

---

# 2. Strategic Positioning

ODF is not a replacement for governance.

OMG is not a replacement for delivery.

Both work together.

ODF answers:

"How do we build AI-powered solutions faster?"

OMG answers:

"How do we govern AI-powered assets safely?"

---

# 3. Core Problem We Are Solving

Organizations are adopting:

- AI Agents
- LLMs
- Copilots
- AI Models
- AI Workflows
- RAG Systems
- AI Applications

Most organizations cannot easily answer:

- What AI assets exist?
- Who owns them?
- Who approved them?
- Which are high risk?
- Which use sensitive data?
- Which have been validated?
- Which require review?
- Can they be stopped?
- Are they audit ready?

OMG exists to solve this problem.
