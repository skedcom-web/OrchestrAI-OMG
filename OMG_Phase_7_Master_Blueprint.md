# OMG Phase 7 Master Blueprint
## Continuous Monitoring & Governance Review Center
### Version 1.0 (Approved Baseline)

---

# Mission

Phase 7 answers:

"Is the AI system still operating within approved governance boundaries?"

Phase 6 established:

- Operations Center
- Kill Switch Center
- Override Center
- Incident Management
- Retirement Governance

Phase 7 introduces continuous governance oversight.

---

# Strategic Objective

Most governance programs focus on:

- Pre-deployment reviews
- Approval decisions

But governance can deteriorate after deployment.

Examples:

- Risk profile changes
- Evidence becomes outdated
- Compliance obligations change
- Findings remain unresolved
- Operational incidents increase

OMG must continuously monitor governance health.

---

# Core Principle

Approval is not permanent.

Governance must be continuously reassessed.

# Governance Health Engine

Purpose:

Calculate ongoing governance health.

Assessment Areas:

## Ownership Health

Checks:

- Owners Assigned
- Owners Active
- Approvals Current

## Risk Health

Checks:

- Risk Assessments Current
- Critical Risks Reviewed

---

## Validation Health

Checks:

- Validations Current
- Reviews Completed

---

## Compliance Health

Checks:

- Assessments Current
- Controls Reviewed

---

## Operational Health

Checks:

- Open Incidents
- Kill Switch Events
- Override Events

# Governance Health Score

Ownership = 20%

Risk = 20%

Validation = 20%

Compliance = 20%

Operations = 20%

Overall Score = Governance Health Score
