# OrchestrAI OMG – Phase 9 Master Blueprint
## Executive Governance Hub & Policy Governance

Version: 1.0 (Approved)
Status: Phase 9 Build Blueprint

---

# Phase 9 Vision

Transform OMG from an Enterprise AI Governance Operating System into an Executive AI Governance Command Center.

Core Principle:

Governed Decisions.
Minimal Governance Debt.

Phase 9 focuses on:

- Executive Governance Hub
- Policy Governance
- Governance Scorecards
- Governance Health Index
- Executive Oversight
- Board & Regulator Reporting

Not on adding unnecessary assessments or governance layers.

---

# Strategic Objective

A CIO, CRO, CISO, Compliance Officer, Board Member, or Regulator should understand the enterprise AI governance posture within five minutes.
