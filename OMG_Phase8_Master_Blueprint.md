Current navigation is feature-driven:

- Asset Registry
- Ownership Matrix
- Risk Center
- Validation Center
- Evidence Center
- Decision Authority
- Audit Logs

While technically correct, enterprise executives think in governance journeys, not feature lists.

Phase 8 reorganizes OMG around governance workflows.
