# OrchestrAI OMG – Phase 10 Master Blueprint
## Governance Change Management

Version: 1.0 (Final Baseline Edition)
Status: Approved
Product: OrchestrAI Model Governance (OMG)

---

# Phase 10 Vision

Complete the OMG governance lifecycle by introducing Governance Change Management.

Core Principle:

Approval is not permanent.

Any significant change to an AI asset must be evaluated, governed, and approved before it proceeds.

---

# Why Phase 10 Exists

Current OMG can answer:

- What AI exists?
- Who owns it?
- What risks exist?
- Who approved it?
- What evidence exists?
- What policies govern it?

However, mature enterprises eventually ask:

- What changed?
- Who approved the change?
- Did the change require reassessment?
- Did the risk profile change?
- Did policy obligations change?
- Should the asset be reapproved?

Phase 10 addresses these questions.

---

# Strategic Objective

Govern not only AI Assets.

Govern AI Changes.
