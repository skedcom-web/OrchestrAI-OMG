# Decision Readiness Engine

Purpose:

Automatically evaluate governance readiness.

Evaluation Areas:

## Ownership

Checks:

- Business Owner Assigned
- Technical Owner Assigned
- Risk Owner Assigned

Status:

Pass / Fail

---

## Risk

Checks:

- Risk Assessment Complete
- Risk Tier Assigned
- Critical Risks Reviewed

Status:

Pass / Fail

---

## Validation

Checks:

- Required Validations Complete
- Validation Results Available

Status:

Pass / Fail

---

## Evidence

Checks:

- Evidence Available
- Evidence Approved
- Evidence Traceable

Status:

Pass / Fail

---

## Findings

Checks:

- Critical Findings Resolved
- High Findings Addressed

Status:

Pass / Fail

---

# Decision Recommendation Engine

Purpose:

Generate a recommended outcome.

Possible Outcomes:

## GO

Requirements:

- Ownership Complete
- Risk Complete
- Validation Complete
- No Critical Blockers

---

## CONDITIONAL GO

Examples:

- Medium Findings Open
- Additional Monitoring Required
- Temporary Controls Needed

---

## NO GO

Examples:

- Missing Ownership
- Missing Validation
- Critical Findings Open
- Missing Evidence

---

# Human Decision Authority

System recommends.

Human decides.

Required Fields:

- Decision Owner
- Decision Date
- Decision Outcome
- Decision Justification

# Governance Score Engine

Purpose:

Provide executive visibility.

Scoring Areas:

Ownership = 20%
Risk = 20%
Validation = 20%
Evidence = 20%
Findings = 20%

Example:

Ownership 100
Risk 100
Validation 80
Evidence 90
Findings 70

Overall Score = 88

---

# Governance Readiness Status

90 - 100
Ready

70 - 89
Conditionally Ready

Below 70
Not Ready
