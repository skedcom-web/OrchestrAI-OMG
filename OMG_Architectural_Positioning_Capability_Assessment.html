Official OMG Positioning

OMG (OrchestrAI Model Governance) is intentionally positioned as a Governance Intelligence & Governance Operations Platform.

It is not intended to replace IAM platforms, runtime control planes, AI gateways, workflow engines, SIEMs, security platforms, execution platforms, or enterprise orchestration platforms.

OMG establishes governance state, evidence, ownership, accountability, reassessment, traceability, governance continuity, and governance intelligence.

Customer-specific runtime enforcement, identity integration, execution controls, operating-model implementation, workflow integration, and enterprise deployment patterns are intentionally delivered through configuration and ODF-based extensions according to organizational requirements.

Executive Summary

Assessed against what OMG actually claims to be

OMG's governance domain modeling is real, deep, and verified directly against code: governance continuity, reassessment, evidence, findings, corrective actions, certification, readiness, a working reasoning engine, an interactive Governance Studio, and a genuine dual-mode (evaluation / production) data architecture. Measured against its stated mission — a Governance Intelligence & Governance Operations Platform, not an IAM platform, runtime enforcement platform, AI gateway, workflow engine, SIEM, security platform, cloud platform, multi-tenant SaaS platform, enterprise control plane, or agent runtime — the platform is coherent and largely does what it says it does. The handful of genuine gaps that remain sit entirely inside that stated mission: evidence sufficiency, authority currency, a composite governability verdict, admissibility assessment, dynamic reassessment triggering, cross-signal governance intelligence, and governance record portability. Nothing outside that mission is treated as a gap in this report.

Most Important Section

What Are Real OMG Gaps?

Only genuine OMG Core evolution opportunities appear here — each one sits inside OMG's own stated mission.

1Evidence Sufficiency

computeEvidenceReadiness()'s evidenceExists check is presence-only (assetEvidence.length > 0) — no minimum count, no type-matching, no relevance-to-classification check. A genuine gap inside the Evidence & Traceability mission.

2Authority Currency

authorityProfileCompleteness() counts non-empty name fields; it never re-derives whether the named authority is still current. Inside the Continuity/Intelligence mission — distinct from identity verification, which is correctly out of scope.

3Governability Status

Health score, gates, drift, and continuity are each computed independently; nothing composes them into one live, explainable governability verdict. The highest-leverage opportunity identified in this review.

4Admissibility Assessment

No mechanism diffs the conditions present when a decision or certification was granted against current conditions — a natural extension of the already-real Decision Reconstruction capability.

5Dynamic Reassessment Triggering

The 13-type trigger taxonomy and its automatic downstream state-flip both work correctly; no reachable human-facing entry point to actually file a new trigger was found, and no automatic detection exists.

6Governance Signal Intelligence

Individual signals (findings, drift, policy violations, outcome tiers) are each real and correctly computed, but cross-signal correlation — recognizing a pattern across multiple simultaneous signals as more significant than any one alone — goes only as far as the Drift engine's own Compound Escalation rule. Broader governance signal intelligence beyond that one rule is not yet built.

7Governance Record Portability

Beyond one JSON export button and four browser-print dialogs, no structured export or portability tool exists for a customer's full governance record set.

Explicitly not listed as OMG gaps — because each belongs to a category OMG does not claim to be: IAM, SSO, Entra ID, Multi-tenancy, Runtime Enforcement, AI Gateway, SIEM, Workflow Engine, Control Plane, Agent Runtime. Their absence from OMG core is correct architecture, not an oversight — see the Official OMG Positioning statement and the Architecture Positioning Diagram above.

Final Executive Conclusion

The honest verdict

OMG is best understood as a Governance Intelligence & Governance Operations Platform.

Its strongest capabilities lie in governance state management, evidence, traceability, accountability, reassessment, governance continuity, and governance intelligence.

The next evolution is not becoming a runtime platform, but strengthening governability intelligence through evidence sufficiency, authority currency, admissibility assessment, governability status, and dynamic governance reasoning — while preserving a clear boundary between governance and execution.

Every classification in this report follows the rule: an item is only called an OMG gap after first determining it belongs to OMG Core — not to configuration, ODF implementation, customer environment, or a category explicitly outside OMG's stated scope.
